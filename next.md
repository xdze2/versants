Core idea is that the territory is not linear, but higlhy folded, each valley is a small world, and going to the neighbors valley could be very difficult even if close on the map

- Tree view with 2 levels:

|-o Selected river [bold]
|- sub river a (+n_b) ..
|- sub river b (+n_b)
